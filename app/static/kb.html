③ Find answers in support chats —

Turn useful answers from past conversations into draft knowledge. Check each draft for accuracy before adding it to the knowledge base.

Find draft answers → Review each answer → Approve and make searchable

Approval saves the answer and adds it to search. Rejection keeps it out of the knowledge base.
