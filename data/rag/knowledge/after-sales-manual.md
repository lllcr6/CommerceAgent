# Paw & Hearth After-Sales and Product Support Guide

## Contacting human support

Use the chat's human-support option or submit a support ticket from the order or ticket area. An AI assistant may explain policies, look up authorized order information, and help prepare a request. Human chat handoff and support tickets are separate services; creating a ticket does not connect a live agent. Do not invent a phone number, email address, mobile app, repair-center address, or store location.

Human-support hours are Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. America/Los_Angeles (Pacific Time, observing daylight saving time), excluding U.S. federal holidays. Requests can be submitted at any time. We aim for a first human response within 1 business day; this is a service target, not a guaranteed queue or resolution time. Weekend and holiday submissions are reviewed the next business day. The demo queue does not enforce business hours or simulate guaranteed staffing.

## What information support needs

Provide the order number, affected model, problem description, and relevant photos if available. Device troubleshooting may require the serial number, but never a password or payment security code. Support must verify order ownership before sharing order details or authorizing a change. If more than one order or product could match, ask which one. A photo can help investigate; an unboxing video is not a mandatory condition.

## Limited electronics warranty

All powered catalog products have a 12-month limited warranty from delivery for defects in materials or workmanship during normal household use. Powered models are MH-LP50, MH-LP100, MH-LP200, MH-W20, MH-W40, MH-W60, MH-HP12, MH-HP20, MH-FD10, MH-FD30, and MH-CAM1. A replacement receives the remainder of the original warranty or 90 calendar days from replacement delivery, whichever is longer. Keep the order receipt; a missing delivery date requires review.

The warranty excludes normal wear, consumables, chewing, accidental damage, unauthorized modifications, and liquid damage caused by use contrary to the model's cleaning instructions. Nonpowered beds, harnesses, leashes, bowls, toys, and cat furniture do not have this electronics warranty; verified defects still go to support for review under the return policy and any applicable rights. Do not infer warranty eligibility from a product name or order status alone.

## Warranty service and repair shipping

Support first troubleshoots the issue and verifies eligibility. An approved claim may be resolved with a replacement part, repair, or replacement unit, at support's direction and subject to availability. Paw & Hearth pays standard shipping both ways for approved warranty service at supported U.S. addresses. Obtain authorization before shipping. Repair turnaround depends on diagnosis and parts; there is no fixed completion deadline. Out-of-warranty repair requires a quoted fee and customer approval before work begins. There is no paid warranty-extension plan.

## Electrical and product safety concerns

Stop using and unplug a device if safe to do so when there is smoke, a burning smell, a damaged cord, unexpected motion, or overheating. Keep pets away and contact human support. Do not recommend repeated restarts or bypassing a safety sensor in these circumstances. The assistant cannot diagnose a pet's injury or illness; direct health concerns to a veterinarian. Do not claim a product is escape-proof, chew-proof, medically therapeutic, or certified to a standard absent evidence.

## Litter box setup and troubleshooting

MH-LP100 and MH-LP200 support the Paw & Hearth device app on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi; MH-LP50 has no Wi-Fi or app features. The device app is a fictional product feature, separate from this project's customer-support website. Use only the documented cat weight range and use manual mode for kittens under 6 months. Place the unit on a firm, level floor. For a routine full-bin alert, empty and reinstall the bin with power disconnected. If motion or sensor behavior seems unsafe, discontinue use and contact support rather than bypassing safeguards.

## Fountain setup, cleaning, and no-flow checks

For MH-W20, MH-W40, and MH-W60, unplug before cleaning, check water above the minimum line, and inspect the removable pump intake for debris. Clean the bowl and pump weekly; replace the model-specific filter every 2-4 weeks, sooner if visibly dirty. Never submerge a power adapter or a component not marked washable. If a cord or pump is damaged, stop use and contact support. Filters for MH-W20 and MH-W40/MH-W60 are different and are not interchangeable.

## Feeder and camera connectivity

MH-FD10 is an offline scheduled feeder. MH-FD30 and MH-CAM1 use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi; 5 GHz-only networks are unsupported. Feeders take dry kibble 2-12 mm across, not wet, raw, or freeze-dried food. Unplug before clearing a jam. Food portions vary with kibble; measure actual output rather than promising grams per portion. Check power and network settings for routine connectivity issues, and never ask for the Wi-Fi password in chat. Neither feeder guarantees feeding during an extended outage.

## Walking gear, beds, and toys

For MH-DH20 harnesses, measure the dog's chest at the widest point behind the front legs and use the size chart. A dog's weight or breed is insufficient to select a size. Inspect harnesses and MH-DL10 leashes before each walk; damaged gear must be replaced. Beds have washable covers and nonwashable foam cores. Toys and slow feeders require supervision; remove damaged parts and do not promise indestructibility. A poor fit within the return window is reviewed as a fit return, not automatically a defect.
