# Paw & Hearth Billing and Shipping

## Currency, prices, and stock

All store prices, shipping charges, discounts, and refunds are in U.S. dollars (USD). Listed merchandise prices exclude sales tax. Applicable sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping destination and items. There is no universal sales-tax rate in this knowledge base. Prices and stock are operational data, not fixed policy: quote them only from a current USD-configured product or order record. Do not convert an old CNY demo amount by relabeling it USD. An unknown price or stock level must remain unknown.

## Payment and payment privacy

Accepted store-policy payment methods are Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay where displayed at checkout. We do not offer buy-now-pay-later financing, checks, wire transfers, cash on delivery, or gift cards. A failed payment does not establish a completed order. A pending bank authorization is not proof that a settled charge or duplicate charge occurred; support must investigate. Never request full card numbers, CVV codes, passwords, or one-time login codes in chat or tickets.

## Receipts and billing questions

Order confirmation and itemized receipts show merchandise, discounts, shipping, sales tax, and order total. Customers can view their order details or ask support for help obtaining a receipt. We do not issue VAT invoices or require a tax identification number for an itemized receipt. A suspected duplicate charge requires a verified order and payment review; do not promise a refund solely from a screenshot or a pending authorization.

## Shipping destinations

Delivery is available only to street addresses in the contiguous 48 U.S. states and Washington, DC. Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, APO/FPO/DPO military addresses, PO boxes, and international addresses are unsupported. There is no physical store pickup or same-day delivery. Support cannot override an unsupported destination or quote an undocumented remote-area surcharge.

## Standard shipping charges

Standard shipping is free when the order's merchandise subtotal is at least $49 after all merchandise discounts and Paw Points rewards, before sales tax. Otherwise standard shipping is $4.95 per order. All current catalog products qualify; there is no oversized-item or rural-area surcharge for supported addresses. The fee is per order even if we split it into multiple packages. Example: $52 minus a $5 reward leaves $47 of merchandise, so $4.95 shipping applies. Sales tax does not count toward the threshold.

## Processing, transit, and business days

In-stock orders normally ship in 1-2 business days after payment confirmation. Standard transit is estimated at 3-5 business days after carrier pickup. Neither estimate is a guaranteed delivery date. Business days are Monday-Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays; the human-support schedule is separate. Weekends do not count toward processing, transit, or refund business-day estimates. Do not promise a particular arrival date without verified order-specific evidence.

## Tracking, split shipments, and delays

Tracking becomes available after the carrier receives shipment information; a label-created status does not prove carrier pickup. A split order may have multiple tracking numbers and delivery dates without an extra shipping fee. Check each package before treating an item as missing. Carrier choice and stock must come from order data; no carrier is guaranteed in advance. If tracking has not updated for 3 business days after carrier pickup, contact support for investigation. This is a contact guideline, not a refund deadline.

## Address changes and cancellations

Cancellation or address correction may be requested before warehouse processing starts, but is not guaranteed. Unshipped does not necessarily mean editable. Once processing starts or a package is handed to the carrier, support must review available options. Delivered orders use the return policy. Item quantities or variants cannot be edited in an existing order; an approved cancellation and a new order may be needed. A cancellation request is distinct from confirmed cancellation.

## Delivered but not received

Check the delivery location, household, neighbors or building office where appropriate, and any carrier delivery photo. If the package is still missing, contact support with the order number. Support investigates carrier scans and may approve a replacement or refund after review. There is no automatic replacement, police-report requirement, or guaranteed carrier-claim resolution time in this policy.

## Out-of-stock products

We do not accept backorders or preorders. If an ordered item becomes unavailable before fulfillment, support contacts the customer about cancellation and refund of that item, including any applicable tax. We do not substitute a different model without consent. There is no guaranteed restock date or implemented restock-notification service.
