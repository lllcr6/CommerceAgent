# Paw & Hearth Paw Points and Promotions

## Free membership and eligibility

Paw Points is a free, optional rewards program for registered customers. There are no paid tiers, annual fees, automatic upgrades, priority human-support guarantees, or membership-specific shipping allowances. Joining does not enroll the customer in recurring shipments. Rewards balances and redemption require a verified account; the assistant cannot invent a balance.

## Earning points

Earn 1 point for each whole $1 actually paid for eligible merchandise after coupons and rewards. Calculate points on the eligible order merchandise subtotal and round down once per order: $24.99 earns 24 points. Shipping, sales tax, refunded amounts, and the portion paid with rewards earn no points. Points post 14 calendar days after the last package in the order is delivered. Returned merchandise reverses its associated earned points; a reversal can produce a negative balance, which future earnings offset.

## Redeeming points

Redeem 100 points for a $5 merchandise discount, in increments of 100 points, up to 300 points ($15) per order. A $5 redemption requires at least $5 of remaining eligible merchandise after coupons; a $10 redemption requires at least $10, and a $15 redemption at least $15. Rewards cannot pay for shipping or tax and have no cash value. The $49 free-shipping threshold is calculated after the reward discount, so redeeming points can make shipping payable. Rewards cannot be transferred to another account.

## Expiration and returns

Unused earned points expire 12 months after they post; oldest points are redeemed first. On a full order refund, all redeemed points are restored. For a partial refund, the order's recorded line-item reward allocation determines the restored points; do not guess the allocation. Restored redeemed points keep their original expiration or remain valid for 30 calendar days after restoration, whichever is later. Refunds return only the amount actually paid for items; rewards are restored as points rather than cash. Support handles missing points and allocation disputes.

## Coupons and promotions

One promotional code may be used per order. A valid code can be combined with Paw Points, subject to its disclosed eligible items, minimum spend, and expiration. Code minimum spend is measured on eligible merchandise before that code's discount and before rewards, excluding shipping and tax. Promotions apply only while valid and do not guarantee future availability or restocking. Do not invent a welcome discount or coupon code. There is no blanket rule that every offer stacks with every sale.

## Price adjustments

A one-time own-store price adjustment may be requested within 7 calendar days after order placement for an identical model and variant. Clearance, flash sales, coupon-only offers, bundles, and competitor pricing are excluded. Support verifies the original paid price and qualifying lower price before approving a refund of the difference and any applicable tax adjustment. Redeemed points are not paid out as cash. The 7-day adjustment period starts at order placement, not delivery.

## Subscription and account limits

Paw & Hearth does not offer Autoship, automatic replenishment, recurring charges, paid memberships, or birthday gifts. This demo describes the intended rewards policy, but has no rewards ledger, redemption checkout, or payment integration. Support may explain these rules or record a question; it cannot claim to credit points, apply a coupon, change a subscription, or complete a price adjustment without an implemented tool and verified success.
