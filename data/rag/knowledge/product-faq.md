# Paw & Hearth Shopping FAQ

## About Paw & Hearth

Paw & Hearth is an online-only pet supplies brand for cats and dogs. We sell litter boxes, fountains, feeders, home accessories, walking gear, and enrichment toys. We have no retail stores, pharmacy, veterinary service, grooming service, or pet insurance. We do not sell food, treats, medications, live animals, or gift cards. Products and policies in this project describe a fictional demonstration retailer.

## What is your return policy?

Most products can be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery. Self-cleaning litter boxes MH-LP50, MH-LP100, and MH-LP200 have a separate 60-calendar-day in-home trial after delivery. Clean, gently tried products with all included parts are accepted; opened or used consumables are excluded from change-of-mind returns. See the Returns and Refunds Policy for eligibility, shipping fees, and exceptions. A policy explanation is not a return approval.

## How do I request a return or refund?

Open the relevant order in My Orders and submit a return/refund request, or contact support through chat or a support ticket. Include the item and reason. Support checks eligibility and provides return authorization and shipping instructions. Do not mail items to an unverified address. Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved or that money has been sent.

## Do you offer exchanges?

For a different size, color, or model, return the original item under the return policy and place a separate order. Prices and availability for the new order may differ. A replacement for a verified defective, damaged, or incorrect item is handled by support and is subject to availability.

## Where do you ship, and how much is shipping?

We ship to street addresses in the contiguous 48 U.S. states and Washington, DC. Standard shipping is free on a merchandise subtotal of $49 or more after discounts and rewards, before sales tax. Below $49, standard shipping is $4.95 per order. We do not ship to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, APO/FPO/DPO addresses, PO boxes, or international addresses. See Billing and Shipping for timing and delivery exceptions.

## When will my order arrive?

In-stock orders normally leave the warehouse in 1-2 business days. Standard transit is estimated at 3-5 business days after carrier pickup. These are separate estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Tracking and an actual order lookup are needed for order-specific updates. Weather, holidays, and carrier disruptions can cause delays.

## What payment methods and receipts do you support?

Our store policy supports Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay where offered at checkout. All prices and charges are in USD. An itemized receipt is available with the order. The project does not process real payments. Never send full card details or security codes in chat.

## Can I change or cancel an order?

Support can request cancellation or an address correction before warehouse processing starts. An unshipped order may already be processing, so cancellation is not guaranteed. After carrier handoff, use the return process rather than assuming cancellation is available. Do not refuse delivery without support instructions. Our order tools do not establish warehouse processing status; support must verify it.

## What if tracking says delivered but I cannot find my package?

Check the delivery location, household members, building office, and carrier delivery photo if available. Contact support if it is still missing; support can investigate with the carrier. Do not assume theft, promise an automatic refund, or ask the customer to place a duplicate paid order as a condition of investigation.

## Do you match prices or offer price adjustments?

We offer a one-time adjustment if the same model and variant drops in price on our own store within 7 calendar days after the order was placed. Contact support with the order and lower price. Clearance, flash sales, coupon-only offers, bundles, and other retailers' prices are excluded. The adjustment window starts at order placement; the return window starts at delivery.

## Are rewards or subscriptions available?

Paw Points is our free rewards program. Eligible merchandise earns 1 point per $1 actually paid; 100 points can redeem $5 on a future eligible order. Full details are in Paw Points and Promotions. There is no paid membership, Autoship, recurring billing, or subscription service.

## Can you recommend a product for my pet?

We can compare the models in our catalog using documented dimensions, features, and intended use. Harness fit depends on chest measurements, not breed alone. Fountains and feeders do not replace daily pet care. We cannot diagnose illness, prescribe treatment, or guarantee a product will resolve a medical or behavioral condition.
