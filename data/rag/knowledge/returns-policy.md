# Paw & Hearth Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard 30-day returns

Request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery for products purchased directly from Paw & Hearth. The delivery date is day 0; the last eligible request date is the delivery date plus 30 calendar days. The request date, not the warehouse receipt date, determines whether the request is within the window. An order placement date or payment date cannot substitute for a missing delivery date.

Most durable products may be opened and gently tried, but must be clean, free of pet waste and excessive hair, undamaged, and returned with all included parts. Ordinary fit checks are allowed for harnesses, leashes, and beds. Damage from chewing, misuse, or modification is not a change-of-mind return. Normal packaging can be opened; the original box is preferred but equivalent protective packaging is accepted. Missing eligibility facts require clarification or support review, not automatic approval.

## Self-cleaning litter box 60-day trial

MH-LP50, MH-LP100, and MH-LP200 have a 60-calendar-day in-home trial after delivery instead of the standard 30-day window. Delivery is day 0 and the last eligible request date is delivery plus 60 calendar days. A cleaned, gently used litter box can qualify if the cat does not adapt. Remove litter and waste, clean and dry the unit, and include all parts. Consumable litter or filters are not included in the trial. This trial is distinct from the 12-month electronics warranty; it does not extend other products' return windows.

## Consumables and exclusions

Unused, sealed replacement fountain filters, odor cartridges, and waste-bag rolls qualify for standard 30-day returns. Opened or used consumables cannot be returned for change of mind. Defective, damaged, or incorrect consumables still qualify for support review; the sealed-item restriction does not block those claims. No current catalog item is final sale. Any future final-sale exclusion must be explicitly disclosed before purchase. Purchases from another retailer must be handled through that retailer for returns.

## Return authorization and dispatch

Request help through My Orders, chat, or a support ticket, identifying the order, item, reason, delivery date, and condition. Support verifies ownership and eligibility, then provides return authorization and a prepaid label where applicable. Ship the authorized return within 14 calendar days after authorization. Contact support if the label expires or the deadline cannot be met; exceptions require review. Do not send an item before receiving instructions or invent a return address. Use protective packaging and retain the carrier receipt.

## Return shipping fees

For change-of-mind or fit returns, the prepaid label fee deducted from the refund is $4.95 per authorized return shipment for standard items, or $25 per authorized return shipment containing a self-cleaning litter box. The $25 fee replaces, rather than adds to, the $4.95 fee for that shipment. Support confirms grouping and the fee before authorization; separately authorized shipments can incur separate fees. There is no additional restocking fee. Original outbound shipping is not refunded for a change-of-mind return. We do not retroactively charge outbound shipping when a partial return drops the kept merchandise below $49.

For a verified defective, transit-damaged, incorrect, or missing item, Paw & Hearth covers necessary return shipping and does not deduct a return-label fee. A missing item does not require mailing back an item never received. If the entire order is returned because of our verified error or a verified defect, original outbound shipping is also refunded. On a partial error/defect return, the original outbound shipping charge remains unless support approves an adjustment.

## Refund amounts and timing

After the return is delivered to our warehouse, inspection and refund issuance normally take 3-5 business days. After issuance, the payment provider may need another 5-10 business days to post the credit. These are estimates, not guarantees, and neither period starts when the return request is submitted. An approved cancellation with no return shipment normally has its refund issued within 3-5 business days after cancellation confirmation, followed by the same estimated bank posting period.

Refunds go to the original payment method. Refundable merchandise value is the amount actually paid for the returned items after allocated discounts, plus applicable refunded sales tax, less any disclosed return-label fee. Refunds do not exceed the original amount paid. Bundled merchandise uses the order's allocated line-item prices, not undiscounted retail prices. Earned or redeemed rewards are handled under Paw Points and Promotions. A request, authorization, warehouse receipt, refund issuance, and bank posting are separate events; do not claim a later event without evidence.

## Exchanges and replacements

We do not offer direct size, color, or model exchanges. Return the original item and place a separate new order. Support may approve a same-item replacement for a verified defect, damage, or fulfillment error, subject to stock. Do not promise a substitute, replacement shipment, or refund until approved. Returnless refunds or donations are exceptions only when support explicitly authorizes them.

## Damaged, incorrect, or missing items

Contact support as soon as a delivery problem is discovered. We recommend reporting within 7 calendar days after delivery so we can investigate promptly; this is not a hard cutoff that automatically voids return or warranty eligibility. Keep the packaging and provide photos of the item and shipping label if available. An unboxing video is not required. Support determines whether a replacement, return, or refund is appropriate. Do not ask a customer to operate a damaged electrical device for proof.

## Returns versus warranty

The return and trial windows address eligible returns; warranty covers qualifying defects for 12 months after delivery for powered products. A defect reported after the return window can still qualify for warranty service. Consumables, ordinary wear, chewing, and accidental damage are not covered by the electronics warranty. See the After-Sales and Product Support Guide. These store policies do not limit rights that apply under law; questions about such rights require human review.
